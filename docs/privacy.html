SUMDAY

Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

Sumday does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app contains no networking code, so there is nothing for it to send anywhere.

What the app stores

Sumday saves your game progress on your device: your daily results, your streak, your statistics, how many hints you have used, and your Endless high score.

This is kept in your device's local storage and never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it permanently — there is no backup, and no way for us to recover it.

What we do not do

We do not use analytics. We do not serve advertising. We do not use crash reporting or any third-party SDK that transmits data. There are no accounts, so we hold no email addresses, names, or identifiers of any kind. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Sharing your results

When you tap Share, your device's own share sheet opens with a short text summary of your day. You choose whether to send it and to whom. Nothing is shared unless you send it yourself, and the summary never leaves your device until you do.

Children

Sumday is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it stores or transmits, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the privacy declarations on the App Store and Google Play will be updated to match.
