## Full description

Play and App Store both accept this as-is.

```
Five number puzzles. One target each. Everyone in the world gets the same five.

You're given five tiles and a number to make. Combine any two tiles with +, −, ×
or ÷ and they become their result. Keep going until a tile equals the target.

That's the whole game. It gets hard fast.

Every puzzle has a par — the fewest moves anyone could possibly use. Hit par for
a green square, solve it the long way for a yellow one. Miss it and take the
white square on the chin. Five squares, one grid, share it however you like.

• A new set of five every day at midnight, the same for every player
• Streaks for showing up
• Endless mode when one round isn't enough
• Three hints a day when you're properly stuck, and the board warns you the
  moment a target stops being reachable
• Every result is a whole number, and division is always exact
• No account, no ads, no timers pushing you to buy anything

Works completely offline. Collects nothing about you — no analytics, no
tracking, no network requests at all.
```

## Privacy declarations

Both stores, the same true answer: **no data collected**.

- **Play → Data safety:** No data collected. No data shared. Not required for
  app functionality.
- **App Store → App Privacy:** Data Not Collected.

This is accurate: the app has no network code, no analytics SDK, no crash
reporter, and no account system. Everything lives in on-device storage. If you
later add analytics, you must update both declarations before shipping — a
mismatch is an automatic rejection.

## Privacy policy

Play requires a hosted URL even for apps that collect nothing. Put this on
GitHub Pages, a Notion public page, or any static host, then paste the URL into
both consoles.

```
Privacy Policy for Sumday

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Sumday does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

What the app stores
Sumday saves your game progress — your daily results, your streak, your
statistics, and your Endless high score — in your device's local storage. This
information never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else,
because the app contains no networking code of any kind.

Deleting the app deletes this data permanently. There is no backup and no way
for us to recover it.

What we do not do
We do not use analytics. We do not use advertising. We do not use crash
reporting or any third-party SDK that transmits data. We do not have accounts,
so we hold no email addresses, names, or identifiers. We do not track you
across apps or websites.

Children
Sumday is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including
children under 13.

Changes
If a future version of the app changes what it stores or transmits, this policy
will be updated before that version is released, and the store listing's
privacy declarations will be updated to match.

Contact
Questions about this policy: ajpalanki@gmail.com
```

## Screenshots

**Play:** minimum 2, up to 8. 16:9 or 9:16, at least 320px on the short side.
**App Store:** at least one 6.9" iPhone set (1320 × 2868).

Capture these three, in this order — they tell the story in the right sequence:

1. **Home** with a streak visible. Play a few days first, or temporarily set a
   streak in code to shoot it, then revert.
2. **Mid-puzzle**, ideally with a tile selected and an operator highlighted, so
   the interaction is legible from the thumbnail.
3. **Results grid** with a mix of green, yellow and white squares. This is the
   screenshot that sells the app, because it's the one people recognise.

Don't add marketing captions over them for v1. Clean device screenshots convert
fine and can't be accused of misrepresenting the app.
